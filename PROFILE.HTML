01 / Case Study
Superstore Sales Analysis & Forecasting Dashboard
Retail Analytics · Power BI · Business Intelligence
Power BI SQL Excel DAX Power Query
Problem Statement
  • The retail business had years of sales data sitting idle in spreadsheets with no visual layer. Managers couldn't identify which regions, categories, or customers were driving — or draining — profit.
  • Decisions were delayed because reports took hours to compile manually each week, with no ability to drill into root causes.

Process
1
Imported raw CSV data into Power Query; cleaned nulls, fixed date formats, removed duplicates
2
Built relational data model connecting orders, customers, products & geography tables
3
Wrote DAX measures for YTD revenue, profit margin %, MoM growth, and return rate
4
Designed 3-page interactive dashboard with slicers, drill-through, and conditional formatting
5
Added Power BI's time-series forecasting module for 3-month demand prediction
Key Insights
  • Technology category drove 36% of revenue but had the lowest profit margin — highlighting a pricing issue
  • West region outperformed all others in volume but had 18% higher return rates, suggesting fulfillment problems
  • Top 20% of customers contributed 68% of total profit — classic Pareto pattern, flagged for loyalty strategy
  • Q4 seasonality spike identified: inventory planning suggestions sent to management

02 / Case Study
IPL Multi-Season Performance Analysis Dashboard
Sports Analytics · Data Modelling · DAX Optimization
Power BI SQL DAX Data Modelling
Problem Statement
  • IPL data was scattered across multiple seasons and formats — making it nearly impossible to draw cross-season comparisons, evaluate venue impact, or understand what actually wins matches.
  • Analysts needed a unified view for team performance, player metrics, and match outcome prediction patterns.

Process
1
Ingested multi-season IPL dataset via SQL; cleaned inconsistent team/player naming conventions
2
Designed star schema in Power BI: fact_matches at center, linked to dim_players, dim_venues, dim_teams
3
Created optimized DAX measures: win rate %, strike rate, economy rate, NRR calculations
4
Built multi-audience dashboards: fan view (leaderboards) + analyst view (heatmaps, comparisons)
5
Benchmarked performance — identified report load time drop of 30% after DAX refactoring
Key Insights
  • Toss-winning teams chose to field first 74% of the time — and won those games at a 61% rate
  • Certain venues showed consistent scoring patterns: high-scoring grounds had a measurable impact on team strategy selection
  • Identified top 5 "impact players" across seasons using a composite performance index (runs + wickets + fielding)
  • Powerplay performance was the single strongest predictor of match outcome — surfaced via correlation analysis
